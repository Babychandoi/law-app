# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Vietnamese businesses, founders, and creators who need practical support with intellectual property and related legal services. They often arrive with limited legal knowledge, time-sensitive questions, and a need to understand the next concrete step before committing.

## Product Purpose

Luật Poip Legal explains complex legal and intellectual-property services in clear Vietnamese, establishes professional trust, and turns qualified visitor interest into consultation leads through form submissions, phone calls, and Zalo contacts.

Success means visitors can quickly identify the relevant service, understand its value and process, and confidently contact Luật Poip Legal for consultation.

## Brand Personality

Authoritative, clear, and approachable. The voice should communicate legal expertise without sounding distant, translate complexity into practical guidance, and make asking for help feel straightforward.

## Anti-references

- Flashy gradients that compete with the legal content or weaken professional credibility.
- Excessive animations that distract visitors or slow down decision-making.
- Generic corporate templates that make Luật Poip Legal indistinguishable from other service businesses.
- Dense legal jargon that forces visitors to interpret specialist language before they can act.

## Design Principles

1. **Earn trust before asking for contact.** Show relevant expertise, process clarity, and credible evidence before presenting a lead-generation action.
2. **Translate law into next steps.** Explain legal complexity in plain Vietnamese and always make the visitor's next action obvious.
3. **Keep consultation within reach.** Forms, phone calls, and Zalo contact should remain easy to find without becoming intrusive.
4. **Use authority with restraint.** Professional confidence comes from precise content, hierarchy, and consistency rather than visual spectacle.
5. **Design for uncertain visitors.** Help people who do not yet know which service they need compare options and ask informed questions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.1 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, sufficient color contrast, meaningful alternative text, semantic structure, and reduced-motion preferences. Content should use plain Vietnamese and avoid unnecessary legal jargon so people with different levels of legal knowledge can understand it.
